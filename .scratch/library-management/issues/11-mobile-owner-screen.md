# 11 — Mobile Owner screen

**What to build:** The Owner, on their phone, can view all registered users and their roles and change any user's role between Librarian and Member — promoting trusted users to staff and demoting them back. The Owner role cannot be granted to anyone through the app. The Owner also has access to everything a Librarian can do.

**Blocked by:** 08, 07

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Users screen: list of all registered users with their roles.
- [ ] Promote a Member to Librarian and demote a Librarian back to Member from this screen; `OWNER` is never offered as a target role.
- [ ] The Owner can also reach the Librarian screens (role gating grants Librarian capabilities to the Owner).
- [ ] Verified manually as the Owner through the demo flow.
